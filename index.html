THE HAHN HEROES APP · COMING SOON

Real-world effort.
A hidden-world adventure.

HAHN Heroes turns learning, kindness, and everyday accomplishments into an anime-inspired adventure. Students create their own hero, earn rewards through home and classroom missions, play games with their squad, and discover the mysteries of the Hidden Nexus.

Built for fifth and sixth graders, with parents and teachers supporting the journey.

Play, learn, and power up.

Explore trivia, word challenges, memory games, pattern puzzles, mysteries, and multiplayer squad games. Collect cards, trade with friends, and raise Nexlings—companions that grow alongside your hero.

Real effort earns real progress.

Learning challenges, acts of kindness, and parent- or teacher-approved missions earn points. Use earned rewards to unlock outfits, accessories, collectibles, and upgrades. The economy rewards participation and progress, with no paid purchases.
